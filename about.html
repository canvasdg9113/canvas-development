ABOUT CANVAS · RICHMOND, VIRGINIA

Building in the place
we call home.

Canvas Development is a boutique development firm rooted in the City of Richmond. Every project we take on is here—because this is the city we know, the city we love, and the place we're invested in making even better.

OUR STORY

Experience across
the real estate world.

27 years of collective experience gives us a perspective that reaches far beyond a single part of the development process.

Our background spans mortgage lending, building and construction, infill new development, leasing, property management, mixed-use development, commercial real estate, and residential real estate. That breadth of experience shapes how we see a property—from its earliest possibility to the way it ultimately becomes part of a neighborhood.

We understand that a successful project isn't only about what gets built. It's about how a place is conceived, financed, constructed, experienced, managed, and woven into the city around it.

RICHMOND, BY DESIGN

Local isn't a market.
It's our point of view.

All Canvas projects are located within the City of Richmond, Virginia. Working in one city allows us to know its neighborhoods, understand its character, and approach each site with context.

From individual residences and infill homes to mixed-use and neighborhood-scale development, we want our work to feel distinctly of Richmond—thoughtful about what came before and optimistic about what comes next.
