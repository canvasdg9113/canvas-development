THE RESIDENCE

Where city life
meets the outdoors.

Set on a distinctive triangular lot in beautiful Byrd Park, Three Points is a contemporary three-story home shaped by its unique setting. Just steps from the walking bridge connecting Byrd Park to the Fan, it brings two of Richmond’s most loved neighborhoods together.

Here, the energy of the city lives alongside lakes, open green spaces, mountain biking, and hiking trails. Morning walks, outdoor adventures, and neighborhood favorites are all part of everyday life.

THE LOCATION

Nature at your door.
The Fan just across.

From quiet paths beside the lakes to miles of nearby trails, Byrd Park offers room to explore and recharge. Cross the walking bridge and you’re connected to the Fan’s neighborhood streets and city life. At Three Points, you don't have to choose between them.
