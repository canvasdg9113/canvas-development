THE PROJECT

Rooted in the hill.
Made for the light.

Set in Richmond’s beloved Oregon Hill neighborhood, Hill + Light brings two thoughtfully planned residences together in one distinctive two-family property.

Each floor offers its own three-bedroom, two-bathroom home, balancing the privacy of a separate residence with a strong connection to the surrounding neighborhood. Generous covered porch spaces at the front and rear extend everyday living outdoors.

TWO HOMES, ONE ADDRESS

Room to live.
Space to belong.

With three bedrooms and two bathrooms on each floor, Hill + Light offers flexible, comfortable living across two distinct residences. Thoughtful layouts create places to unwind, entertain, and feel at home in the heart of the city.

INDOOR–OUTDOOR LIVING

Porches made
for lingering.

Large covered porches at both the front and rear invite fresh air and unhurried moments—from a morning coffee to an evening spent with friends. They create a natural connection between home and neighborhood.
