THE RESIDENCE

Old-world charm.
Modern Richmond living.

Custom-built in 2022, 3131 W. Franklin brings together the character of Richmond's coveted Museum District with the ease and refinement of a thoughtfully designed new home.

2022BUILT2,790SQ FT4BEDROOMS3BATHS

DESIGNED FOR LIVING

Timeless character,
without compromise.

Soaring 10-foot ceilings and varied-width Wellborn + Wright white oak flooring establish a warm, elevated character throughout. At the heart of the home, an expansive gourmet kitchen pairs 35 cabinets with a generous center island, gas cooking, farm sink, wine refrigerator and dry bar, flowing naturally into the dining and family rooms.

White oak ceiling beams, architectural accent walls, built-in bench seating, designer fixtures and abundant storage bring craftsmanship and intention to every room.

THE DETAILS

Made for gathering.
Designed to retreat.

The primary suite is a private retreat with a spa-like bath and custom walk-in closet. Upstairs, additional bedrooms feature walk-in closets alongside a luxury hall bath, custom laundry room with quartz-topped sink and a generous walk-in storage closet.

A 12-foot-wide glass wall opens the family room to a covered rear porch and landscaped backyard. A spacious paver patio, privacy fencing and off-street parking for two create a rare private outdoor setting in the city.

THE NEIGHBORHOOD

In the heart of the
Museum District.

One block from Monument Avenue and within walking distance of neighborhood coffee, dining and the Virginia Museum of Fine Arts, 3131 W. Franklin was created to feel deeply connected to Richmond while offering the comfort of modern construction.
